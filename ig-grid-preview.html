Bulk import from your Content Calendar

In Notion, open your Content database as a table, select the rows (and header row), and copy them (⌘/Ctrl + C). Then paste below. Works with columns named Title, Caption or Notes, Status, Date or Publish Date, and Image or URL — matched automatically, in any order. Rows without an image URL will import with a placeholder you can fill in from the Add / Edit tab.

Planner colours

These colours style the planner panel only — the Instagram preview above stays authentic to the real app so it accurately reflects how your feed will actually look.

Hosting this widget:
1. Save this file, rename it index.html
2. Upload free to tiiny.host, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, or Vercel
3. Copy your live URL
4. In Notion, type /embed and paste the URL

How to use this widget

Adding a post

Drag a photo or video onto the dropzone, or click it to browse your files. Prefer linking to something already hosted (Canva, Drive, Dropbox)? Paste that URL in the field below the dropzone instead — using one clears the other.

Your profile photo

Click or drag a photo onto the avatar circle at the top. Already have a hosted image link? Shift-click the avatar to paste a URL instead.

Reordering your grid

Drag any tile in the grid onto another tile to swap their positions — rearrange until the feed reads the way you want it to.

Status dots

The small coloured dot in the corner of each tile shows where a post is in your pipeline — Idea, Draft, Scheduled, or Live. It's for your own planning only and never shows on real Instagram.

Highlights

Click "+ Add highlight" to create one, or click an existing highlight circle to edit its name/cover or delete it.

Bulk import

Already planning in a Notion database? Copy your rows and paste them into the Bulk Import tab instead of re-typing everything by hand — see that tab for the exact column names it recognises.

Saving your work

Everything saves automatically to this browser as you go. Click Save grid any time for peace of mind. Note: your grid is tied to this browser — it won't follow you to a different device or browser unless you're viewing it through the same embedded link.

Making it yours

Head to the Settings tab to recolour the planner panel and find the steps for hosting and embedding this widget in Notion.
